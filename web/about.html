How the atlas is built

OpenNoise is a music genre map and similar-artist explorer inspired by Every Noise at Once, created by Glenn McDonald. OpenNoise is not affiliated with Every Noise or Spotify. Every Noise genre names and artist memberships are used as training labels.

Source What for License

AcousticBrainz Audio descriptors for layout CC0

Results

  • Hidden Every Noise genres with a matching cluster: 210 of 868 (24.2%).
  • With artists’ Wikidata labels held back, 98 of 262 genres have a matching cluster (37.4%). A match means at least 20% intersection over union of artists.
  • Top-ten similar artists sharing a hidden Every Noise label: 21.93% with Discogs styles, versus 21.48% listening-only.
  • The 2D map keeps about 42% of each genre’s ten nearest listening-and-audio neighbours.

Your shape on the map: enter a ListenBrainz username or playlist to highlight its genres and artists on the atlas. ListenBrainz serves public listening stats keyed by MusicBrainz artist IDs to the browser, so no server is needed, and Spotify can be linked to ListenBrainz. Spotify sign-in also works in the browser, but unreviewed apps are limited to five allowlisted users.
