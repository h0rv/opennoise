# Open music discovery foundation

The goal is an independently constructed, reusable music discovery system with
Every Noise's depth and usefulness. The historical site is the behavioral and
evaluation reference. Its names, coordinates, examples, and memberships are
dated observations, not undisclosed Spotify features and not training labels
for an allegedly independent open model.

The deliverable has two parts: a small static music explorer and an offline
library that others can use for representations, overlapping communities,
membership suggestions, neighbors, and representative music metadata.

## Construction stages

1. Resolve artists, recordings, releases, and release groups by stable identities.
   Keep recording credits and release credits separate.
2. Normalize evidence with explicit source, license, scope, date, and missingness.
   Separate musical descriptions, cultural context, listening aggregates, and
   precomputed sonic metadata. Repeated source claims are not independent votes.
3. Fit overlapping representations and memberships. Native observations and
   model suggestions remain different records. A fixed tree is an optional
   browsing view, not the definition of musical genre.
4. Surface community candidates with supporting artists and source evidence.
   Naming, acceptance, merge, split, and rejection require recorded decisions;
   a cluster or recurring string is not automatically a genre.
5. Measure held-out source recovery, independent musical relevance, stability,
   and reference behavior separately. Historical absences are unknown, not
   negative labels. Preserve sparse and cold artists in the denominators.
6. Export stable maps, complete lists, useful music links, and concise evidence
   details. Fit semantic coordinates before moving labels for readability.

## Acceptance gaps

| Output | Evidence required before claiming completion |
| --- | --- |
| Genre granularity | Reviewed overlapping communities across broad families, regions, languages, eras, and evidence volumes; recurring names alone are insufficient |
| Artist memberships | Independent relevance judgments, reference-positive recovery with verified identity bridges, calibrated uncertainty, and cold-artist accounting |
| Neighbors | Cultural and sonic results evaluated separately, then an explicitly evaluated blend; shared tags alone do not establish sonic similarity |
| Map | Stable meaningful neighborhoods, interpretable measured axes where available, readable labels, and complete alternatives for missing positions |
| Representative music | Exact credits, independently assessed musical fit, diversity, and sufficient discography coverage; search results are bounded examples |
| Listening and playlists | A permitted provider path with availability accounting, plus independently assessed central, recent, and emerging selections |
| Reusability | Versioned inputs and artifacts, correct license boundaries, small runnable examples, and instructions that expose missing full-corpus inputs |
| Interface | Minimal design and successful search, full-list access, selection, history, reload, keyboard, and mobile tasks on the actual export |

There is no defensible single percentage for these criteria. A passing checkout,
a large candidate vocabulary, or a high source-reconstruction score cannot
substitute for the remaining musical judgments.

## Source packs

The permissively reusable core can include MusicBrainz core identities and
credits, Wikidata claims, and AcousticBrainz CC0 metadata. Source-specific
capture and projection receipts still matter. MusicBrainz aggregate tags and
genre associations belong in a separate optional CC-BY-NC-SA pack. Their
availability does not make derived models unrestricted for commercial use.
Raw search responses can contain supplementary tags even when a projected
credit record is CC0; publish the permitted projection rather than assigning
one license to the complete raw response.

No audio recordings enter the present pipeline. Precomputed sonic metadata is
an authorized research input, with source age and coverage recorded. Public
listening evidence needs appropriate aggregate support and privacy treatment.

## Research and publication

Experiments write fresh artifacts and preserve earlier receipts. Fixed
confirmation folds can assess a promising method without recycling the first
test as a tuning set. Negative results remain visible. Product promotion needs
evidence for the intended behavior, not merely successful execution.

The repository's canonical release and local research exports have different
input and certification contracts. Experimental exports are not deployed.
See [the inventory and examples](README.md), [parity evidence](../EVERYNOISE_PARITY.md),
and [design requirements](../serving/DESIGN.md).
