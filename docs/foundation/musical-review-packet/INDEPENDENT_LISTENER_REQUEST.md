# Request for independent musical reviewers

We need two independent listeners for a short pilot: review all eight permitted
FMA excerpts in `fma-permitted-listening/` (about 10 minutes including the
forms). Each excerpt needs two separate reviews. Please work independently,
declare familiarity and conflicts, and return the eight completed rows for your
reviewer slot. Preserve your own judgment even when uncertain: `unsure`,
`unsupported`, and audio access failures are valid answers. Do not discuss items
with the other reviewer until both forms are submitted. Use a consistent
reviewer ID; no names are needed in the shared results.

The eight-item FMA excerpt cohort at `fma-permitted-listening/` has a supplied 30-second clip,
source attribution, and a source-declared per-track Creative Commons license.
Judge only what the excerpt supports, report whether the exact clip played, and
do not infer the full track or artist discography from it. These eight examples
were selected for an explicit audio permission path, not as a representative
music sample.

The remaining tasks below are separate, larger review cohorts that can follow
the pilot; they do not change the pilot's eight-item scope.

Other frozen review tasks are available in their subdirectories:

- `fma-validation-query-review/`: compare the three method-blinded candidate
  tracks for each of 240 validation queries. Rate sonic fit and cultural or
  contextual relevance separately; retain ties and “neither.” Candidate method
  names and source genres are kept in a coordinator-only key.
- `fma-validation-membership-review/`: assess each displayed candidate genre
  for its exact FMA track, not for the artist as a whole. Only confirmation
  partition suggestions are displayed; method, score and source annotations
  are hidden. This can inform calibration; a threshold must be frozen before a
  separate, larger independent confirmation cohort (target: at least 1,000
  queries) is reviewed.
- `exact-recording-review-form.csv`: review the 36 exact credited recordings.
  Nine have literal external provider destinations, but availability and
  permission have not been verified. Check provider terms and exact recording
  identity for your region before listening; a listed link does not itself
  grant permission.
- The 60-artist selected-150 task has blank candidate-label and neighbor fields.
  A coordinator must freeze and populate those candidate outputs before those
  judgments can be collected.

The full-FMA 240-track review files are being regenerated after a correction to
their streaming sample strata. Do not distribute or cite the existing
`fma-full/` files until the corrected build has a fresh manifest. FMA numeric
IDs are native identifiers and do not imply MusicBrainz recording identities.
FMA genre tags are track-level source annotations; missing tags mean unknown,
not negative. Region, language, era, playback access, and global
representativeness must remain unknown unless reviewers establish them from
permitted evidence.

Return both independent forms for each assigned item. Keep the original ratings
and disagreements; any adjudication belongs in a separate record. No reviewers
or judgments have been supplied or assumed in advance.
