# Independent musical review

Source reconstruction measures agreement with a source. It cannot decide whether
a genre fits an artist, whether two artists sound related, or whether a recording
is a useful introduction. Those decisions remain an explicit acceptance dependency.
No agent-generated labels or source votes may stand in for independent listeners.

Before collecting judgments, freeze the model, source receipts, candidate roster,
sampling seed, comparison methods, review questions and decision thresholds in a
versioned declaration. Keep the historical Every Noise observations separate from
construction. Report their capture date, identity-bridge failures and unobserved
items; absence from that reference is not a negative musical judgment.

The review roster must include regions, languages, eras, broad/sub/micro candidate
labels, evidence-volume strata and cold artists. Draw samples from the declared
full denominator, retaining failures to resolve identities, obtain permitted
listening access or form a supported recommendation. Aphex Twin and Four Tet are
required navigation examples, not a substitute for these strata. A convenient
English-language cohort cannot support a global-coverage verdict.

Review these tasks separately:

| Task | Question | Necessary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Membership | Does this musical description fit the artist's work, and at what scope? | Exact artist/recording identities, several independently selected credited works, broad/sub/micro distinction and an unsure response |
| Neighbors | Which candidate is the more useful related artist for this query? | Randomized method-blind pairs, separate cultural and sonic questions, popularity baseline, ties and unsupported queries |
| Defining recordings | Does this recording introduce the requested artist or style well? | Exact credits, discography selection limits, fit and diversity judgments distinct from popularity |
| Maps | Do nearby names form a useful neighborhood? | Neighbor relevance, frozen-seed stability, display overlap and unpositioned-list tasks; component packing is not musical distance |
| Listening selections | Can the person hear the intended recording and use the selection? | Permitted provider access, exact recording match, region/date availability, central/recent/emerging roles and explicit unavailable outcomes |

Use at least two independent reviewers per item. Hide method names, source votes,
confidence values and historical reference memberships while collecting musical
judgments. Randomize order deterministically and retain the hidden assignment
key separately. Names and listening destinations needed to identify the music
remain visible; this is method blinding, not anonymous music. Reviewers declare
familiarity and conflicts. An unsure answer is preserved. Record disagreement,
adjudication and the original answers rather than replacing them with consensus.

Compute uncertainty with the artist or leakage-aware recording component as the
resampling unit. Show per-stratum denominators, missing access, abstentions, cold
and unseen labels alongside supported-query metrics. Evaluate confidence and
abstention against these independent judgments before interpreting suggestions
as calibrated musical memberships. Freeze any promotion threshold before viewing
confirmation results; changes require a fresh confirmation cohort.

Frozen independent-review materials were prepared on 2026-10-03 in
`docs/foundation/musical-review-packet/`. They include a seeded 60-artist cohort
from the selected 150-artist denominator, all 36 exact credited recording
examples (nine literal external provider destinations, with availability and
permission unverified), a seeded 240-track cohort from all 109,727 native FMA
tracks, and the separate 240-query validation comparison from a 7,332-query
known-validation denominator. A confirmation-only candidate-membership form is
also present; source labels, ranks and scores stay in coordinator-only files.
These FMA identifiers remain in the native FMA namespace. Region, language and
era are unknown in the FMA and selected-150 rosters. The current full-FMA
sample files are an interrupted pre-refactor build: the streamed selection and
missingness strata have since been corrected, so those files must be
regenerated and manifest-checked before distribution or use as frozen evidence.

A separate eight-item FMA listening cohort has exact local 30-second clips with
source-declared per-track CC licenses, attribution, hashes and two independent
review slots. The clips are selected for their explicit audio permission path,
not for musical representativeness; external provider and regional availability
remain unchecked. No reviewer identities or musical judgments have been
recorded in any packet. Therefore the musical-relevance, defining-recording and
calibrated-membership gates remain unmet. Counts, a working browser, graph edge
recovery and held-out FMA annotations can support narrower technical claims.
They cannot complete this review or certify Every Noise parity.
