FMA descriptor map

A research map of open track descriptors. Nearby points describe similar measured summaries; musical similarity has not been established.

PC1 and PC2 are mathematical axes. Genre points average training tracks with that source annotation. Artist points average native track descriptors; they do not assign genres to artists.
