This subset contains matching supplied license declarations and reviewed credits, not a rights warranty. Local candidate only; no deployment authorized. Original audio bytes remain unchanged. Counts refer to distinct recordings and artists in this subset, not the full FMA catalog. Genre links are supplied track tags, not model predictions.

Metadata: FMA: A Dataset For Music Analysis, Michaël Defferrard, Kirell Benzi, Pierre Vandergheynst and Xavier Bresson; CC BY 4.0. Selected subset and minimized fields; source credits preserved.

The map is a train-only two-dimensional projection of descriptors extracted from these excerpts. Suggested excerpts use raw standardized descriptor similarity, not the map distance. Neither is a validated musical judgment. All recordings remain available in Tracks, including those without coordinates or suggestions.
