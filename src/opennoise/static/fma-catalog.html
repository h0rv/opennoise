Sources and license

Michaël Defferrard, Kirell Benzi, Pierre Vandergheynst, Xavier Bresson, FMA: A Dataset For Music Analysis, ISMIR 2017. Metadata is CC BY 4.0. Recordings have separate artist-selected licenses; no audio is included.

Native FMA IDs remain separate from MusicBrainz IDs. Source track-artist associations and source track genres are metadata observations. They are not artist genre memberships, musical-fit judgments, or model suggestions. The raw tables differ from the cleaned audio dataset.

Connected genres share direct track annotations, ranked by shared tracks divided by tracks annotated with either genre. Up to six connections are shown. These are metadata relationships, not verified sonic similarity. Artist pages summarize their tracks’ annotations without classifying the artist.
