A model of music communities

Broad, sub, and micro communities are model outputs inferred from retained open metadata. Their names are derived from feature descriptors. They are not source taxonomy claims or reviewed genre names.

An artist can have overlapping model memberships. Scores are uncalibrated model similarities, not probabilities or importance rankings. Artists with insufficient evidence remain searchable without modeled assignments or positions.

The source explorer preserves exact artist observations and source identities independently. This preview contains metadata and is local research. View the model provenance receipt.
