Direct observations come from exact MusicBrainz artist records. Model neighbors and artist proposals are inferred and remain separate. Complete source artist pages use alphabetical display order. Older bounded examples use MusicBrainz ID order. Neither order measures importance.

Coordinates use only the modeled overlap graph. They describe a browsing arrangement, not musical truth. Genres without enough overlap stay searchable without coordinates. Map colors distinguish positions for readability; they do not encode acoustic measurements.
